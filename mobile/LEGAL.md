# Bridge - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## Overview

Bridge ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the Bridge mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Information Collection and Use

### Location Information
- **Purpose:** To show you nearby donations, calculate delivery routes, and match drivers with deliveries
- **Retention:** Only while you have an active delivery or session
- **Sharing:** Only with other users involved in the transaction

### Camera & Photos
- **Purpose:** To photograph items for donation listings
- **Retention:** As long as the item listing is active
- **Sharing:** With users viewing the listing

### Contact Information
- **Email:** To verify your account and send transactional notifications
- **Phone:** Optional, for driver identification (never shared without consent)

### Profile Data
- **Name, Email, Profile Picture:** For user identification and ratings
- **Role (Helper/Receiver/Driver):** To provide appropriate app features
- **Stats & Ratings:** To build community trust

## Data Storage

- **Firebase Firestore:** User profiles, listings, deliveries
- **Firebase Storage:** User photos and item images
- **Local Storage:** Cached data on your device (AsyncStorage)

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and local data protection laws.

## User Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Opt-out of non-essential communications
- Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@bridgeapp.com

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase (Google):** Authentication, database, storage
- **Gemini AI (Google):** Image analysis for item detection
- **OSRM:** Route planning
- **Nominatim:** Geocoding services

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Firebase security rules for database access
- Regular security audits
- No storage of payment information (handled by payment providers)

## Children's Privacy

Bridge is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or email.
