### Description

**Short Description (30 chars):**
Peer-to-peer donation platform

**Full Description:**

Bridge connects people with surplus food and clothing to people nearby who need it—instantly, safely, and with volunteer drivers handling delivery.

**The Problem:**
Food banks have friction on both sides. People with extra stuff don't bother donating because drop-off is inconvenient. People in need don't have transportation to get there. Waste sits next to need with nothing in between.

**The Solution:**
A peer-to-peer app where neighbors help neighbors:

1. **Helpers** post what they have to donate
2. **Receivers** browse nearby offers or request items
3. **Drivers** accept deliveries on demand

Once matched, everyone gets live delivery tracking and a shared chat—no phone numbers shared, no hassle.

**AI-Powered:**
Just take a photo of what you're donating. Our AI reads the image and builds your listing automatically—turning a 2-minute chore into 10 seconds.

**Three Roles, One Community:**
- **Helper:** Post surplus items (food, clothing, household goods)
- **Receiver:** Browse local offerings or request what you need
- **Driver:** Earn helping others with flexible delivery gigs

**Safety First:**
- Real user profiles and ratings
- All deliveries tracked live
- Private messaging (no direct contact sharing)
- Community guidelines enforced

Bridge transforms generosity into action. Start helping today.

donation, community, food, clothing, delivery, social network, peer to peer, charity, helping, neighbors
